Business Day Convention:

A “business day” is any day, other than a Saturday or Sunday, which is not a day on which banking institutions in the City of New York, Toronto, Ontario, Canada, or Vancouver, British Columbia, Canada are authorized or required by law or executive order to close.

 

Following Business Day Convention:

If not a business day in New York, Toronto or Vancouver, then payment of a coupon or upon maturity or redemption will be made on the next business day with no adjustment.
